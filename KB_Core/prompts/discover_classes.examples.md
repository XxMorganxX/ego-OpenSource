Never output a theme or idea (`demand forecasting`, `supplier onboarding`, `my reading
list`), a specific named thing (`Vandelay`, `Acme`, `Orchard Labs`), a narrow
implementation detail (`pull request`, `test case`, `config key`, `file`), or a fragment
copied from prose. Reject conjunctions, verbs, adjectives, values, misspellings,
repeated words, schema terms, and meta labels such as `type`, `types`, `category`,
`entity`, `untyped`, `new`, `example`, or `n/a`.

Use the broad parent when one fits: a quarterly review belongs under `event`; a supplier under
`organization`; a warehouse under `place`; a mobile app under `system`.
