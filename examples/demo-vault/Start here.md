# Start here

This is an invented demo vault for ego-OS, and everything in it is fictional: Orchard Labs, its people, its customer Bramblecrest Grocers, its vendor Glacierline Cold Storage, and its tools Larchwire, Pomewise and Brightpost Pager were all made up for this example, and any resemblance to real organizations or people is accidental. Point ego-OS at this folder to try ingestion and search on safe data before you bring your own notes.

## The story

Orchard Labs tracks fruit from harvest through cold storage to shipment with its [[Fruit-Inventory Service]]. In early 2026 the team normalized the inventory schema, fixed a duplicate-SKU import bug, integrated a cold-storage vendor feed, survived a feed outage and approved an append-only audit trail. In March they started [[Ripeness Forecast]], a pilot with the grocery chain Bramblecrest Grocers.
