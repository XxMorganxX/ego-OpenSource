# Bramblecrest Grocers kickoff

Attendees: [[Avery Quinn]], [[Sam Rivera]], [[Jules Okafor]] (Bramblecrest Grocers)

Kickoff for the [[Ripeness Forecast]] pilot.

## What Bramblecrest wants

Jules opened with last summer's numbers: about 9% of stone fruit written off across their stores, mostly peaches that arrived ripe and sat for two days. They want a "days to ripe" figure per pallet on the delivery note, nothing more.

## What we can offer

- Avery explained that every pallet already carries a harvest batch and a cold-storage temperature history, thanks to the [[2026-02-05 release notes|v1.4 release]].
- Sam proposed starting with a simple rule (fruit type, harvest date, average cooler temperature) and only building something smarter if the rule misses badly.

## Agreed

- Three pilot stores, chosen by Jules.
- Pilot runs June to August. Success means 10% less shrink on stone fruit at those stores.
- Store staff do a ripeness spot check on two crates per delivery and record it on a paper form; Jules will send photos of the forms weekly.

## Action items

- [ ] Jules: name the three pilot stores by March 15.
- [ ] Sam: draft the rule and test it on last summer's batches.
- [ ] Avery: send a one-page summary of the pilot to Jules.
