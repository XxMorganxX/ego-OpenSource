# Jules Okafor

Produce operations manager at Bramblecrest Grocers, a regional grocery chain and one of Orchard Labs' largest customers. Main contact for the [[Ripeness Forecast]] pilot. Email: jules.okafor@example.com

## What Jules cares about

- **Shrink.** Fruit thrown away in store because it ripened too early or too late. Bramblecrest writes off about 9% of stone fruit each summer.
- **Predictable deliveries.** Stores want to know two days ahead what will arrive ripe.
- **One number per pallet.** Jules does not want a dashboard, just "days to ripe" on the delivery note.

## History

- Long-time customer of the [[Fruit-Inventory Service]]; asked for batch-level traceability, which helped settle the [[2026-01-10 schema decision|schema decision]].
- Kicked off the pilot with [[Avery Quinn]] and [[Sam Rivera]] at the [[2026-03-04 Bramblecrest Grocers kickoff]].

## Notes

Prefers a short written update every second Friday. Jules shares the store-level shrink report on the first Monday of each month.
