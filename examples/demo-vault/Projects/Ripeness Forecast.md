# Ripeness Forecast

A pilot that predicts how many days each pallet of stone fruit has until it is ripe enough to sell, using the cold-storage temperature readings already flowing into the [[Fruit-Inventory Service]] and the harvest date of each batch.

- **Customer:** Bramblecrest Grocers, contact [[Jules Okafor]]
- **Product:** [[Avery Quinn]]
- **Pipeline:** [[Sam Rivera]]
- **Platform:** [[Dana Whitfield]]

## Goal

Cut shrink on peaches, plums and apricots by 10% across three Bramblecrest stores during the summer pilot, compared with the same stores last summer.

## Approach

1. Start with a simple rule: days to ripe from fruit type, harvest date and average cooler temperature. No model until the rule has been beaten by something measurable.
2. Print "days to ripe" on the delivery note. Nothing else is shown to stores.
3. Compare predicted and actual ripeness from store spot checks every week.
